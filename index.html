threejs-extras

Small fixes for things I ran into while building a gallery editor in three.js. Each one has a demo.

light-slots Adding a lamp freezes the view while every lit shader recompiles. This keeps the light count steady. spot-beam A visible spot light beam that fades into the wall and floor, where the usual transparent cone cuts them with a hard line. lean-frame One multisampled target and one output pass instead of the composer chain, at a capped pixel ratio.
